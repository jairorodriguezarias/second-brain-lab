# Project — Personajes de la AI (historia + laboratorios)

**Status:** active · **End:** a who's-who catalog of AI history and the labs that drove it (deck or booklet)

## Objective
A teachable, citable "who's who in AI" — the people who founded the field and the labs where the breakthroughs happened — so a student can answer "¿quién es quién en la IA?" in one place, from Turing to the transformer authors to today's frontier lab leaders.

## The through-line (the Hinton tree)
One professor (Geoffrey Hinton, Toronto) → three revolutions:
- **AlexNet** (2012, Krizhevsky + Sutskever, Hinton's students) — ImageNet, halved the error rate, launched deep learning for vision.
- **Backprop + Boltzmann machines** (1983/1986, Hinton) — the foundations neural nets train on today.
- **Transformers** (2017, Vaswani et al., Google Brain) — "Attention Is All You Need", the architecture behind every modern LLM.

Six of Hinton's students/affiliates came out of one Toronto department. The "OpenAI diaspora": Sutskever → SSI, Amodei → Anthropic, Schulman → Anthropic, Karpathy → Eureka, Murati → startup.

## The founding trio (2018 Turing Award)
**Bengio (Montreal/Mila) · Hinton (Toronto/Vector Institute) · LeCun (NYU/Meta)** — "fathers of the deep learning revolution."
- Hinton: backprop, Boltzmann machines, AlexNet, dropout.
- Bengio: word embeddings + attention (2000), GANs (with Goodfellow).
- LeCun: convolutional neural networks (1980s), early backprop, broad vision for nets as modular automatic-differentiation systems.

## The labs (where the frontier lives)
- **OpenAI** (2015, San Francisco — Altman + Sutskever + Musk founding; GPT line; ChatGPT (Nov 2022) = the inflection point of public AI.
- **Anthropic** (2021, San Francisco — ex-OpenAI (Amodei + others); Constitutional AI; Claude line.
- **Google DeepMind** (2014, London, acquired by Google) — AlphaGo (2016), AlphaFold (2020), Gemini.
- **Google Brain** (2011) — the transformer paper (2017); merged into DeepMind (2023).
- **Meta FAIR** (2015, New York — LeCun VP & Chief AI Scientist; Yann LeCun; Llama line.
- **Mila** (Montreal) — Bengio's institute; the largest academic deep-learning center.
- **Stanford SAIL Lab** — Fei-Fei Li (ImageNet), Christopher Manning, Percy Liang.

## The 2024 Nobel pivot
Physics: **Hinton** + **John Hopfield** (Hopfield nets, Boltzmann machines — the roots of deep learning).
Chemistry: **Demis Hassabis** (DeepMind) + John Jumper + David Baker — AlphaFold (protein folding).
